📈 Project S-Curve

Cumulative value over time showing Planned (PV), Earned (EV), and Actual Cost (AC)

📖 What Do These Metrics Mean?

SPI (Schedule Performance Index)

≥ 1.0: Ahead of schedule ✅
< 1.0: Behind schedule ❌

CPI (Cost Performance Index)

≥ 1.0: Under budget ✅
< 1.0: Over budget ❌

EAC (Estimate at Completion)

Forecasted total cost when project is complete.

VAC (Variance at Completion)

Difference between budget and forecasted final cost.
Positive: Under budget ✅
