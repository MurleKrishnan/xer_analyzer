📖 What Do These Metrics Mean?

SPI (Schedule Performance Index)

≥ 1.0: Ahead of schedule ✅
0.95 – 0.99: Slightly behind ⚠️
< 0.95: Significantly behind ❌

CPI (Cost Performance Index)

≥ 1.0: Under budget ✅
0.95 – 0.99: Slightly over budget ⚠️
< 0.95: Significantly over budget ❌

EAC & ETC

EAC: Forecasted total cost at completion (BAC / CPI).
ETC: Forecasted remaining cost (EAC − AC).

VAC (Variance at Completion)

Difference between original budget and forecasted final cost (BAC − EAC).
Positive: Forecasted under budget ✅
