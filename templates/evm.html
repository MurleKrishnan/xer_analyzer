📖 What Do These Metrics Mean?

SPI (Schedule Performance Index)

≥ 1.0: Ahead of schedule ✅
0.95 – 0.99: Slightly behind ⚠️
< 0.95: Significantly behind ❌

CPI (Cost Performance Index)

≥ 1.0: Under budget ✅
0.95 – 0.99: Slightly over budget ⚠️
< 0.95: Significantly over budget ❌

Peak Labor Month

The month with the highest planned man-hour demand. Useful for resource leveling and crew planning.
